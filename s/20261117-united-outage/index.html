  17. c18 · Norma explains (analysis)

    The pilots' union said United could not give departing flights their final weights, and the Guardian notes that the aircraft's weight is part of the preflight check that a plane can take off safely. That is consistent with what United described: delayed departures, while flights already in the air were unaffected. Passengers said crews fell back on older methods to get the numbers. What failed, and why, United has not said.
