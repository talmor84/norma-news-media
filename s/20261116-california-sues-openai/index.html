  24. c22 · Norma explains (analysis)

    California is using two existing state laws, on unfair competition and public nuisance, to hold an AI developer responsible for how it tests its agents and for what those agents did. The state says OpenAI lacked safeguards and misled the public. OpenAI says the suit appears to single it out over a challenge every major lab has identified, and other labs have had similar incidents. How legal liability applies in such cases is still for courts to decide.
