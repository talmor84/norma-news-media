  22. c30 · Norma explains (analysis)

    The plan keeps police at protests, through a 30-officer support team and a patrol unit that can make arrests, but puts unarmed civilians first. Supporters say it goes further than the 2023 settlement; critics, including some Jewish leaders and police unions, say protests will be harder to control. It takes effect over 90 days, so how it works in practice is not yet known.
