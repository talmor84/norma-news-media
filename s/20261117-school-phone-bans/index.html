  23. c23 · Norma explains (analysis)

    Both studies looked at schools that lock phones in pouches. The new one finds small gains, about 1 to 2 weeks of learning a year, and its authors say they could add up over time. The earlier study found close to zero overall. Whether the gains keep adding up is the open question; an author of the earlier study says a few years of data are not enough to extrapolate.
